Why WGF

Built Different.
For Girls Who Are.

01
🏈

NFL-Affiliated

We run the official NFL FLAG program — that means real jerseys you keep, professional refs, and a curriculum backed by the National Football League.

02
⚡

Trained Parent Coaches

Teams are parent-coached, guided by the official NFL Flag rules and curriculum. It's not a free-for-all — it's structured skill development from coaches invested in their own kids' team.

03
🎯

Two Practices. Real Games.

Monday & Tuesday practices, Sunday games. No 4-night-a-week commitment. Flag football complements your other sports — it doesn't compete with them.

Age Divisions

Find Your Level.
Own Your Field.

K–2
Grades K–2 · Rookie Division
Introduction to the fundamentals. Big on fun, light on pressure. You'll learn how football works and leave every practice with a smile.
3–4
Grades 3–4 · Junior Division
Routes, reads, and real competition. You know the basics — now build your game. Multi-sport athletes love this level for the skill transfer.
5–6
Grades 5–6 · Varsity Division
Where athletes become football players. Deeper playbooks, higher intensity, and the foundation for competitive play. This is where it gets serious.
7–8
Grades 7–8 · Elite Division
Competitive play with tournament pathways including NFL FLAG Regionals. Leadership roles, travel team potential, and the clearest view of what's ahead in high school.
