Frequently Asked Questions

Does she need prior experience?

No. All skill levels are welcome — most girls are trying flag football for the first time.

What if she plays another sport too?

Perfect — one practice a week is designed to complement other activities, not compete with them.

Can I volunteer or coach?

Always. Email westportgirlsflag@gmail.com and we'll follow up with options.

When does the season run?

Primarily fall (Sept–Oct/Nov), with spring programming available.

Ready to Join the Team?

Spots fill up fast, especially in 5th-8th grade divisions.
